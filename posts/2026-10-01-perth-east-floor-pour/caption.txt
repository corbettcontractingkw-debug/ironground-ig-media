Big pour, early start 🔥

📍 Location: Perth East, Ontario

🏗️ Project: Steel building floor, subcontracting for @gulutzenconcrete

👷 Scope of Work: Insulation board, slab on grade, placing and finishing

📝 Project Description: Our crew subbed in with Gulutzen Concrete Construction on a full slab inside a new steel building, started before sunrise. Rigid insulation board went down first, then the pump placed the concrete and a laser screed set it level ahead of the crew. Power trowelled smooth with saw cut control joints for a clean floor built to take the weight.
